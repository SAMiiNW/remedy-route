RemedyRoute

An evidence-guided route from a documented issue to a selected, independently supported remedy.

Open contract in GenLayer Explorer

Workflow

  1. Open a route with issue details, remedy tracks, and two distinct sources.
  2. Select the evidence-supported remedy through consensus.
  3. The assigned recipient submits a separately hosted completion record.

Use it

Call open_route, select_remedy, and submit_completion in Explorer. Read status with get_route.
